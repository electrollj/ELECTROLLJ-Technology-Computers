Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about the ELECTROLLJ demonstration website.

Can I buy products on this website?

Not yet. This is a demonstration storefront. Product listings and USD prices are illustrative, and checkout does not process real orders.

Are the products and specifications real?

The product names, visuals, and prices are fictional concepts used to demonstrate the site.

Does the cart work?

The cart and wishlist work locally in your browser using localStorage. No purchases or payments occur.

Do you offer repairs or IT support?

These are planned business concepts only. No operational support service is being represented at this stage.

Does the contact form send messages?

No. It is a front-end demonstration and does not transmit messages.

How can I follow ELECTROLLJ?

Visit our GitHub profile at github.com/electrollj for future updates.
